SA CoParents Parenting Self-Assessment

Take a moment to reflect on how your current parenting and co-parenting relationship is working for your child.

There are no perfect scores. The purpose is to identify strengths, areas that may need attention, and opportunities for moving forward.

Start with your situation. Focus on your child. Move toward alignment.

How to Complete the Assessment

Answer each statement based on your experience during the recent parenting relationship.

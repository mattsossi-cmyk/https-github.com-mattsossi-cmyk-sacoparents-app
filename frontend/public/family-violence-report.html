Recommended Communication Pathway

Tier 2 · Structured Written

Structured written communication is the safest starting point.

Based on Jane’s responses, direct verbal or in-person communication with the other parent is not recommended at this time. A monitored written channel reduces reactive escalation and preserves a record of coordination decisions.

  • Use OurFamilyWizard, TalkingParents, or a similar co-parenting app for schedule, medical, and school updates.
  • Avoid live phone or in-person exchanges for the next 90 days.
  • Route emotionally-charged topics through a mediator, therapist, or attorney — not directly.
  • Re-take this assessment every 90 days to track whether conditions are changing.

Interpretation

Jane’s Section 1 answers surface patterns consistent with coercive control — fear, monitoring, and prior controlling behaviour — without recent physical violence or an active protective order. Her Section 2 answers indicate that boundary-setting and emotional safety with the other parent are currently very difficult, but a strong personal support system exists and she is open to structured written communication.

This combination places her in the Tier 2 — Structured Written pathway rather than a No-Direct-Contact recommendation. If Section 1 answers change (e.g. any “Yes” on physical harm, weapons, or child harm), the pathway would automatically escalate to Tier 4 — No direct contact — and Jane should immediately consult a family-violence-trained attorney.

How this report was generated

This assessment adapts questions from the Danger Assessment (Campbell, 1986) and the HITS screening tool (Sherin et al., 1998). Scoring is deterministic and rule-based; no AI classification is used to assign a pathway. Any “Yes” response on the Section 1 immediate-safety questions triggers a stricter pathway regardless of Section 2 answers.

This is a screening tool, not a risk assessment. It should not be used in court proceedings without accompanying professional evaluation. See sacoparentsapp.online/methodology for the full scoring rules.

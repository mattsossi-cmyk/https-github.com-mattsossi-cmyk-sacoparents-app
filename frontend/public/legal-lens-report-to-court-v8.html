6

Conflict Resolution

What do the identified differences mean for resolving the dispute, and what process may help the parents address those differences?

Parent Discussion / Agreement Building
Appropriate where parents demonstrate substantial alignment and can address remaining differences directly.
Structured Communication
Consider where disagreement appears connected primarily to communication patterns rather than substantive parenting goals.
Child-Focused Mediation
May assist parents in examining differing perspectives through the child's developmental and practical needs.
Traditional Mediation
May assist where defined legal or parenting disputes are ready for negotiated resolution.
Professional Consultation
Educational, medical, mental-health, or other appropriate professional input may help narrow specific disagreements.
Parent Facilitation / Parenting Coordination
Where authorized and appropriate, structured professional involvement may assist with repeated implementation or communication disputes.
Separate Safety Pathway

Family Violence / Safety Considerations

Family-violence or safety concerns should not be treated simply as a higher level of ordinary co-parenting conflict.

Where identified, the case should follow the applicable SA CoParents safety assessment and safeguarding pathway.
